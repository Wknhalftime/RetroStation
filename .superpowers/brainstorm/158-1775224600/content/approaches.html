Calendar Upgrade: 3 Approaches

How should the drill-down calendar (Year → Month → Day) be structured?

A

In-Place Zoom (Recommended)

The existing calendar card transforms between three views. Click the "2024" header → shows year grid. Click a year → shows month grid for that year. Click a month → shows day grid. Back button to go up a level.

Pros

  • Minimal UI footprint — same card, same sidebar space
  • Familiar pattern (iOS/Android date pickers work this way)
  • Only active years/months shown — no dead clicks
  • Smooth transition, easy to understand hierarchy

Cons

  • Can't see day grid and year grid simultaneously
  • Requires clear "zoom out" affordance so users don't get lost
B

Sidebar Accordion

Replace the calendar with an expandable tree: Years as top-level items, expand to see months, expand a month to see available days. Click a day to select it.

Pros

  • Can see multiple levels at once
  • Very compact for sparse data
  • Familiar file-tree pattern

Cons

  • Loses the visual calendar grid — harder to see "which days of the week"
  • Long accordion if many years with many months
  • Less visually engaging than a calendar
C

Dual Panel: Year List + Calendar

Add a year/month selector panel above or beside the existing day calendar. A compact horizontal row of available years, then month chips below. Day grid stays visible at all times.

Pros

  • Day grid always visible — best for frequent day selection
  • Quick year/month jumping without losing context

Cons

  • Takes more vertical space in the sidebar
  • More complex layout to get right
  • May feel cluttered with many years
