Navigation Flow

  • Zoom in: Click a year → see months. Click a month → see days. Click a day → select it.
  • Zoom out: Click the header text at any level (e.g., "January 2001" → month grid, "2001" → year grid).
  • Month-to-month: Chevron arrows still work at the day level for adjacent month navigation.
  • Data-driven: Year grid only shows years with data. Month grid dims months without data. Day grid dims days without data (existing behavior).
